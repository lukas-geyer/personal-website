Muninn now runs as a real app on the iPhone. It is in development and not on the App Store yet; this entry is updated as the app grows.

### Today

- **One Clear Target:** The first screen shows how many cards are left today, how many of them are due and how many are new, and roughly how long they will take. One button starts studying everything.
- **Carry On Where You Stopped:** The decks you studied last are listed underneath, and the full deck list is one tap away. Decks with sub-decks can be folded.

### Studying

- **Four Answers:** Again, Hard, Good and Easy, each showing when the card would come back.
- **Multiple Choice You Can Tick:** Tap the options you think are right and Muninn shows which were right, which were wrong and which you missed.
- **Image Occlusion, Cloze and Typed Answers:** Covered parts of an image, gaps in a text and answers you type are all built in as card types.
- **Liquid Glass and Dark Mode:** The controls follow the look of iOS 26, in light and dark, in English and German.

### Anki Decks

- **Import:** An Anki deck file (`.apkg`) is converted into Muninn's own card types, including home-made multiple choice and image occlusion that many decks build by hand. Review history and due dates come along.
- **Not Yet:** Decks exported in Anki's newest file format have to be exported with "Support older Anki versions" ticked.

### Up Next

A card browser, statistics, creating and editing cards, and export. The Mac app follows once the iPhone app feels right.
