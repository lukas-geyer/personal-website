Paradeiser 1.0 is the first version. Development started on 21 November 2026; it is finished in code and not on the App Store yet. This entry is updated when it ships.

### The Timer

- **Your own intervals:** Any combination of focus blocks and breaks, such as three 45-minute blocks with 15-minute breaks, saved as presets.
- **Keeps running:** The timer follows the clock, so it stays right when the app is closed or the iPhone is locked.
- **Live Activity:** The running interval on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, with its progress, the time left and buttons to reset, pause, resume and skip.
- **Chimes and notifications** when a round or a break ends.
- **English and German.**

### Semestra and Paradeiser

- Paradeiser works together with [Semestra](/semestra/), the study planner. When a study block is coming up there, Paradeiser suggests focus and break times that fit it, and the focus time counts as studied in Semestra. Free, without an account, and only on your device.

### Paradeiser Plus

- **Tomatoes and streaks:** Every focus round of 10 minutes or more earns tomatoes; days in a row build a streak.
- **Rest Days:** Up to two missed days don't break the streak. Earned every seven days or bought with tomatoes.
- **Tomato Market:** Collect varieties and export them as stickers.
- **Statistics:** Focus time per day and week, the harvest basket, milestones and a weekly recap.
- **Focus tags and a daily goal.**
- **Apple Calendar and Apple Health:** Log finished sessions as events and as Mindful Minutes.
- **More app icons, accent colors and chimes**, unlocked by milestones.
- **Three ways to get it:** monthly, yearly with a free week, or a one-time purchase.
