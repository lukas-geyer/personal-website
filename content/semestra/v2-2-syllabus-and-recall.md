This release turns static course syllabi into structured study material from day one of the semester.

### Highlights

- **Syllabus Scanning:** Snap a photo or import a course PDF to automatically extract exam dates, assignment deadlines, lab sessions, and recurring lecture hours.
- **AI Lesson Breakdowns & Active Recall:** Convert syllabus topics into concise summaries, practice quizzes, and flashcard matching sessions tailored to upcoming exams.
- **Spaced Repetition & Audio Playback:** Review concepts you're most likely to forget—or listen to natural-voice lesson recaps while commuting.
