Paradeiser 1.0 is the first release of a calm, tactile focus timer built for deep study and work—keeping the timer front and center without clutter.

### What's Built

- **Custom Intervals & Quick Presets:** Set any combination of focus blocks and breaks with one-minute precision (like three 45-minute study blocks with 15-minute breaks in between) and switch schedules right from the top Liquid Glass pill menu.
- **Lock Screen & Dynamic Island Live Activities:** Follow your active focus or break countdown directly on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, backed by a wall-clock engine that survives backgrounding and app restarts.
- **Focus Statistics (Paradeiser Plus):** Track daily and 30-day focus minutes, streaks, and a day-by-day session timeline showing exactly when and how long you focused.
- **Native Polish & Bilingual Support:** Designed around Apple's Liquid Glass with a custom layered tomato icon, foreground completion chimes, accidental-reset protection, and full English & German localization.

### Up Next

A dedicated macOS companion app sharing the same CloudKit-ready SwiftData models so presets and focus statistics stay in sync between iPhone, iPad, and Mac.
