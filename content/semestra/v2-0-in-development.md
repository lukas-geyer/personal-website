Version 2.0 collects everything built since the first release. It is finished in code and not on the App Store yet; this entry is updated when it ships.

### New for Everyone

- **Guided start:** From your timetable to your first study plan in a few steps.
- **New tabs:** Plan, Exams, Statistics and Settings. Timetable and calendars moved into Settings.
- **Week view:** Lectures and study blocks from Monday to Friday at a glance.
- **Registration deadlines:** Add when you have to register for an exam, and Semestra reminds you in time.
- **Study time from ECTS:** A suggestion for how much to study for an exam.
- **Missed a class?** One tap, and the catch-up time goes into your plan.
- **Streaks, rethought:** They count days you kept your plan. Days off no longer break them.
- **Readiness and milestones:** Every exam shows how much of its study time is done, and milestones mark your first steps, a week on plan and your first fully prepared exam.
- **Redesign:** A calendar like the Calendar app, tidier settings, wheels instead of plus and minus, and better layouts with large text.

### Semestra and Paradeiser

- Semestra works together with [Paradeiser](/paradeiser/), the interval timer. Start a study block in Paradeiser, and the focus time counts as studied in Semestra. Free, without an account, and only on your device.

### Semestra Plus

- **Three ways to get it:** monthly, yearly with a free week, or a one-time purchase.
- **Topics and spaced reviews:** Split exams into chapters; every block says what to work on.
- **Focus timer, widgets and controls:** Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets with Start and Done buttons, a Control Center control and a Focus filter.
- **Grades and ECTS:** Grading scales for Austria, Germany and Switzerland, a weighted average and degree progress.
- **Retakes:** After a failed exam, more study time is suggested for the next attempt.
- **Exams from text or a photo**, read on the device.
- **Also:** statistics, a semester recap to share, PDF export, app icons and accent colors, and iCloud sync.

### Improved

- Timetables are read more reliably, the daily study limit always holds, and the plan is right when the clocks change.
