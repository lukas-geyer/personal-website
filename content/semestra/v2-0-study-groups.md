Semestra 2.0 brings collaboration into the study workflow without turning your planner into a noisy social feed.

### Highlights

- **Shareable Semesters:** Send your course timetable to classmates with a share code or directly via Messages and WhatsApp.
- **Study Groups:** Coordinate exam prep in focused course groups and exchange flashcard decks or practice quizzes.
- **Semestra Plus:** Optional upgrade adding ECTS and grade tracking, detailed study statistics, focus timers, and clean PDF schedule exports.
