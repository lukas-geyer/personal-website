Semestra 1.0 is the first public version. It takes the timetable you already have and plans your study time around it.

### What's in It

- **Timetable in, plan out:** Subscribe to the calendar link from your university portal or import an `.ics` file. Semestra finds the free time in your week and spreads the study time you need in blocks until exam day.
- **Exam detection:** Exams in your timetable are recognised and added with one tap. Each exam gets its study hours, preparation time and priority.
- **A plan that keeps up:** When a lecture or an exam moves, the plan adjusts by itself. Finish, move or skip a block and it recalculates.
- **Preferred study times:** Tell Semestra when you study best, keep your lunch break free and set a daily limit.
- **iPhone calendar:** Appointments stay free, and study blocks can go into a calendar of their own.
- **Reminders and Live Activity:** A reminder before each block, and the running block on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
- **German and English**, light and dark mode.
- **Private by design:** No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your device.
